Privacy Policy for Secure Notes

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Welcome to Secure Notes ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your data completely secure. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, how local device authentication is handled, and your responsibilities regarding device access.
1. Zero-Knowledge & Local Storage

Secure Notes operates on a strict offline-first, zero-knowledge architecture:

    No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or any personal details to use the application.

    100% Local Sandboxed Storage: All your notes and data are stored strictly within the app’s internal local storage on your device.

    No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote databases or servers to store, view, transfer, or sync your notes.

2. Device Authentication (expo-local-authentication)

Secure Notes relies exclusively on your device's native security framework via expo-local-authentication (such as Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or your system Device PIN/Pattern/Password):

    No Access to Biometric Data: The application only receives a boolean status (success or failure) from the operating system indicating whether local verification was successful.

    OS-Level Security: We never capture, view, process, or store your fingerprints, facial recognition data, or system passcode. All authentication is executed solely within your device's secure hardware enclave.

3. Device Lock & Password Loss Disclaimer

    Important Notice on Device Passcodes and Biometrics:

    Secure Notes relies entirely on your device's native lock screen credentials and biometric settings for access. We do not manage, store, bypass, or recover any device lock credentials.

    We will not be held responsible or liable for any loss of device passwords, PINs, pattern locks, or biometric failures that prevent you from accessing your device or your notes. If you are locked out of your device, forget your system credentials, or uninstall/reset the application, your locally stored notes cannot be recovered or restored by us. You are solely responsible for maintaining access to your device.

4. App Permissions

The application requests only the minimal system access required to operate:

    Biometric / Device Hardware: Required exclusively to trigger the system authentication prompt when you open or unlock the app.

    No Extra Device Permissions: The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, external shared media/files, or location.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing & Analytics

We do not track you, run background advertising trackers, or collect any personal usage data. We do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

Secure Notes does not collect, transmit, or process personal data from any users, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if new features or framework updates are introduced in future releases. Any updates will be reflected here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
